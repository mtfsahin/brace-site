Privacy policy

Effective 22 October 2026.

Brace has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no server that stores anything. Its only network requests are for Strava, and only after you connect Strava yourself (see below). The one exception is official technique pages and videos, which load from their publisher, and from YouTube for videos, only when you open them; Brace sends nothing about you with them.

Workouts, routines and settings

They are stored on your devices: on your iPhone and in its backup, and on your Apple Watch, which passes the workouts you log there to your paired iPhone. We never receive them. You can export your training yourself as CSV; it goes only where you send it. Files you import from other apps are read on your device and are not sent anywhere.

Apple Health

Only if you turn it on, Brace saves your workouts to Apple Health. On iPhone it also reads your workouts there, only to recognize workouts your Apple Watch already saved, so none is saved twice. If you connect Apple Health for readiness, it reads your heart rate variability, resting heart rate, heart rate, breathing rate, sleeping wrist temperature, sleep stages, cardio fitness (VO2 max), workouts other apps saved, and your date of birth and sex, to work out your readiness, training load, sleep, night signals and cardio fitness on the device, and to give the on-device coach context. On Apple Watch it reads your heart rate and active energy during a workout to show them and save them with the workout. Health data is used only for that, stays on your devices, is never sent to us or anyone else, and is never used for advertising. To stop saving workouts, turn off Save workouts to Health in Brace's settings. To stop Brace reading Health data, change its permissions in the Health app.

Apple Intelligence

The workout debrief, the routine builder and Ask Brace use Apple's language model on your iPhone. What they read from your log and from Health is processed on the device and is not sent to us or anyone else.

Strava

Optional. Only if you connect your Strava account in Brace's settings, Brace sends Strava the workouts you post there, or every finished workout if you turn on posting them automatically: the workout's name, start time, duration and a description listing its exercises, sets and new records. When a post got no answer, Brace reads your Strava activities from around that workout's time, only to see whether it arrived, so it is never posted twice. Brace contacts Strava for nothing else. Signing in to Strava, and renewing that sign-in, passes through a small relay we run on Cloudflare that adds Brace's Strava app key: it handles only the sign-in codes and tokens, and the basic profile Strava returns with them, such as your name, on their way to and from Strava. It never sees your workouts and does not store or log any of it. The sign-in then stays in your iPhone's keychain, not in a backup. What you post is handled under Strava's own privacy policy. Disconnecting in Brace's settings revokes Brace's access; activities already posted stay on Strava until you delete them there. Health data is never sent to Strava.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are handled by Apple. We do not see your payment details.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app deletes what it stored on your devices. Disconnect Strava in Brace's settings first: that revokes Brace's access and removes the saved sign-in, which can otherwise stay in your iPhone's keychain. Workouts already saved to Apple Health or posted to Strava, and files you exported, stay there until you delete them.

Contact

Questions about privacy: write to github.com/mtfsahin/brace-site/issues, or see the support page.
