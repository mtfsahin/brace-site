Privacy policy

Effective 16 October 2026.

Brace has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no server of its own. It makes no network requests of its own.

Workouts, routines and settings

They are stored on your devices: on your iPhone and in its backup, and on your Apple Watch, which passes the workouts you log there to your paired iPhone. We never receive them. You can export your training yourself as CSV; it goes only where you send it. Files you import from other apps are read on your device and are not sent anywhere.

Apple Health

Only if you turn it on, Brace saves your workouts to Apple Health. On iPhone it also reads your workouts there, only to recognize workouts your Apple Watch already saved, so none is saved twice. If you connect Apple Health for readiness, it reads your heart rate variability, resting heart rate and last night's sleep to work out your readiness score on the device. On Apple Watch it reads your heart rate and active energy during a workout to show them and save them with the workout. Health data is used only for that, stays on your devices, is never sent to us or anyone else, and is never used for advertising. You can turn it off in Brace's settings or in the Health app at any time.

Apple Intelligence

The workout debrief, the routine builder and Ask Brace use Apple's language model on your iPhone. What they read from your log is processed on the device and is not sent to us or anyone else.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are handled by Apple. We do not see your payment details.

Deleting your data

Delete the app to delete everything it stored. Workouts already saved to Apple Health stay there until you delete them in the Health app.

Contact

Questions about privacy: write to github.com/mtfsahin/brace-site/issues, or see the support page.
